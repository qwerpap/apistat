Privacy Policy – Capo Power

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Grepittoos ("we", "us", "developer") handles information in the mobile application Capo Power (package name com.pumra.track), available on Google Play (the "App").

1. Who we are

The App is developed and published on Google Play by Grepittoos. Contact: support@apistatsync.xyz.

2. Information you enter

Workouts, exercises, sets, weights and other training notes you enter in Capo Power are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload them to our servers and we do not have access to them. Deleting the App removes this data.

3. Information collected automatically

To keep the App stable, Capo Power uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC). If the App crashes, Crashlytics may collect crash logs, device model, operating system version, app version and an installation identifier. This data does not identify you personally and is used only to find and fix errors. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. What we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number or other contact details
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Health data from other apps or devices
  • Contacts, photos or files

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except the crash diagnostics processed by Firebase Crashlytics as described above.

6. Data retention and deletion

Workout data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall Capo Power. Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. To request deletion of any data related to you, email support@apistatsync.xyz.

7. Children

Capo Power is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Security

Data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
